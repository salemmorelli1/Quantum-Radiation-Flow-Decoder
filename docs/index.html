Conditional bijection

Likelihood without a partition function.

log qθ(x | c) = log φ(z) + log |det Dₓ fθ⁻¹(x; c)|
c = (τ, Mτ), z = fθ⁻¹(x; c)

Every coupling block transforms half the coordinates using scale and translation functions of the complementary half and the time-mass context. Bounded log-scales prevent singular Jacobians.

Exact

Sample-wise log density

The inverse and Jacobian give exact log q for each continuous telemetry sample, up to floating-point arithmetic.

Estimated

Shannon entropy

Entropy is an expectation of exact log densities and is therefore Monte Carlo estimated unless a closed form is available.

Not implied

Quantum recovery

Classical invertibility alone neither reconstructs phases nor proves reversibility of a black-hole quantum channel.

Scientific safeguards

What this framework does—and does not—claim.

It does: formalize a conditional density model, evaluate Page benchmarks, expose exact change-of-variables terms, quantify uncertainty, and test paired toy-state recovery.
It does not: simulate quantum gravity, identify von Neumann entropy from one measurement basis, or turn network invertibility into a theorem of unitary evaporation.
